# Charm Painter Sigil

Charm Painter Sigil is an offline iPhone rune-drawing puzzle game. Spirits descend through a dark ritual chamber, and each one shows a weakness. The player must draw the matching charm in the lower sigil box to damage it before the seal breaks.

## Gameplay Facts
- 50 local stages.
- Front spirits show rune weakness and HP.
- Flame, Frost, and Rift are attack runes.
- Ward restores HP and does not damage spirits.
- Correct rune matches trigger hit effects; wrong shapes fizzle.
- Progress and settings are stored locally on device.
