Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Charm Painter Sigil is an offline single-player rune drawing puzzle game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information Collection

Charm Painter Sigil does not collect personal information. The app does not include account registration, social login, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party purchase services.

Local Game Data

The app may store unlocked stages and Music or Sound Effects settings locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Children's Privacy

Charm Painter Sigil is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Internet, Ads, and Purchases

  • No internet connection is required for gameplay.
  • No advertisements are included.
  • No in-app purchases are included.
  • No user tracking is included.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.
